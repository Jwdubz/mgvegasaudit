What your site is costing you — Machine Guns Vegas —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a group planning a Las Vegas shooting experience would: find you, pick a package, book. The foundation is solid: every page has its own title, description and canonical address, you publish business markup and an llms.txt file, and booking runs through FareHarbor. The leaks are a very slow homepage on a phone, a “Free pick up” button that doesn't apply to outdoor packages, outdoor pages that never say where the range is, and three phone numbers with no labels.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your homepage takes about 10 seconds to show its main content on a phone.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage transferred 7,824 KiB in 156 requests, its main content showed at 10.6 and 11.4 seconds, the page was blocked from responding for 2,980 and 1,670 milliseconds, and the speed score was 35 and 36 out of 100. 115 of the requests, 3,540 KiB, came from third parties such as ad, analytics and chat scripts. The Indoor Packages page scored 37 and the Seal Team 6 page 49. The Outdoor Packages page scored 35 with its main content at 37.2 seconds in one run. One test setup, two runs on the homepage; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your “Free pick up” button appears on pages where pickup isn't free.

The top bar of every page I opened, including the Outdoor Packages page and the $199, $299 and $399 outdoor pages, has a “FREE PICK UP” link. Your homepage tile says “FREE TRANSPORT WITH ALL INDOOR PACKAGES OVER $200”, and your FAQ says complimentary Uber pickup is for the indoor range and that outdoor guests reach the range by Uber/Lyft (about $80 round trip) or their own car. A visitor on an outdoor page could read the button as covering them. I didn't book anything, so I can't say what happens at checkout.

03

Your outdoor package pages never say where the outdoor range is.

The footer of every page I opened lists one address, 3501 Aldebaran Ave, labeled “Indoor Range”. The Outdoor Packages page and the $199, $299 and $399 pages say the shoot is in the Mojave Desert, but none gives a town, an address or a drive time. The only page I found that does is your FAQ, which says the outdoor range is in Boulder City, about 30 minutes from the Strip. I opened four outdoor pages, not all of them.

04

Your homepage logs a script error and sets 51 third-party cookies.

In the same test, the browser logged an error from chat.satis.fi, a chat-popup script (“Cannot read properties of null (reading 'replace')”), reported 51 third-party cookies and 2 deprecated-code warnings. One script from causalfunnel.com accounted for 1,649 milliseconds of the page's blocked time. The best-practices score was 57 on all five pages I tested.

05

Your site lists three phone numbers and doesn't say what each is for.

The header shows (800) 757-4668. The footer of every page lists (800) 757-4668, (702) 539-5016 and (702) 202-3760, each as a tap-to-call link, with no labels. Your FAQ names 1-800-757-4668 and 702-539-5016 for setting up pickup. Your business markup lists only (800) 757-4668. I didn't dial any of them, so I can't say which one is answered fastest.

06

Some text, slider dots and embedded frames fail the automatic accessibility check.

The accessibility score was 87 on the homepage and 86 or 87 on the other pages I tested. The check flagged low-contrast text in the customer reviews and in the Google Maps review links, slider dots that are smaller than the recommended tap size, and embedded frames with no title. This is an automatic check, not a person using a phone.

What's working

Every one of the 15 pages I opened has its own title, description, canonical address and a single page heading, and the SEO score was 100 on every page I tested. Your homepage carries LocalBusiness markup with your name, phone and address, you publish an llms.txt file (updated October 17, 2026) and a sitemap index, and your robots.txt blocks only admin, author and paging pages. Packages, prices and booking buttons are on the page, and booking runs through FareHarbor.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks on a phone (untested), whether the FareHarbor booking and the WhatsApp link work end to end, your Google listing and reviews as Google shows them, and what any shop employee says when one of the three numbers is called. I didn't book anything, call anyone or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt lists a sitemap and blocks only /wp-admin/, /author/ and one paging parameter. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    llms.txt published

    /llms.txt returns a 2.5 KB file with your tagline, search address and recent articles, dated October 17, 2026. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 1,650 words and one clear heading, so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Business markup

    The homepage has LocalBusiness markup with your name, phone (800) 757-4668 and the address 3501 Aldebaran Avenue, Las Vegas 89102.

  • Gap
    Outdoor range isn't described as a place

    Your markup and footers carry only the indoor address. The outdoor pages say “Mojave Desert” without a town or address, so the Boulder City detail only exists on the FAQ page.

  • Note
    Free pickup wording differs by page

    The “Free pick up” link, the homepage tile and the FAQ describe pickup three different ways, which matters when a search tool quotes one of them.

  • Note
    One image per page has no alt text

    Every page I opened has exactly one image without alt text.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
